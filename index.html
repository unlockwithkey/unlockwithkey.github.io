Questions

Frequently asked

What is credit repair?

Credit repair is the process of reviewing your credit reports and addressing items that may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unverifiable, using the rights consumers have under federal law.

Do you guarantee a specific score increase?

No. No one can ethically guarantee a score increase, deletion, or loan approval. Results depend on each individual’s credit file and circumstances.

Why do I need three-bureau credit monitoring?

It lets us see your reports from Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion in one place, so nothing is missed.

How long does the process take?

Timelines vary by file. We’ll walk you through realistic expectations during onboarding.

Can I work with you if I have evictions or broken leases?

Yes, but if you have a bankruptcy, eviction, or broken lease, you’ll need a consultation before signing up for credit repair. Please contact me directly so we can talk through your situation.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about credit repair, pricing, and the homeownership program.

What is credit repair?

Credit repair is the process of reviewing your credit reports from all three bureaus and addressing items that may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unverifiable, using the rights consumers have under federal law.

What do I need to get started?

You choose a plan, complete a short onboarding form, and enroll in three-bureau credit monitoring. We’ll walk you through any additional information during onboarding.

Why do I need three-bureau credit monitoring?

It lets us see your reports from Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion together, so nothing is missed. Monitoring is $29.99 per month and is paid directly to the monitoring provider. It is required before we begin.

How long does the process take?

It depends on your credit file. For Basic Repair we typically recommend about three months, and you can continue longer if you choose. Dispute rounds typically run 30 to 45 days, and you’ll receive an update each cycle. Individual results vary.

Do you guarantee a specific score increase?

No. No one can ethically guarantee a score increase, the removal of any item, or a loan approval. Results depend on each person’s credit file and circumstances.

Can I work with you if I have evictions or broken leases?

Yes, but if you have a bankruptcy, eviction, or broken lease, you’ll need a consultation before signing up for credit repair. Please contact me directly so we can talk through your situation.

How do I sign up?

Select Get Started, choose your plan, enter your details, enroll in credit monitoring, and review and accept the agreement. Payment is due at sign-up. Once work has started on your profile, there are no refunds. Questions? Email moorewealthfirm@gmail.com or use the Contact page.

What happens after I sign up and pay?

After you sign up, you will receive a welcome message with onboarding instructions and your payment details. Once work has started on your profile, there are no refunds. Your credit monitoring enrollment is confirmed before work starts.

What is the difference between a one-time fee and a payment plan?

A one-time fee is paid once and covers your program. A payment plan is a smaller amount to start, then monthly payments for as long as you choose to continue. Payment plans are available on Basic Repair. Express Repair is one-time fee only.

Do you offer couples pricing?

Yes. Couples pricing is available for both Basic and Express Repair. You can see it on each plan’s tab on the Credit Repair page.

How do I qualify for the 90-Day Homeownership Program?

It is for people who are realistically ready to buy within about six months and whose main obstacle is credit. Review the checklist on the program tab, then book a consultation. Admission is by approval.
